Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Helpful answers before you request a cleaning quote.

How much does a cleaning service cost?

Pricing depends on the type and size of the space, its current condition, the service required and how often you need cleaning. Send an enquiry with these details and Hygello will discuss a suitable quote with you.

What areas does Hygello serve?

Service availability depends on your location and the job. Share your area or full service address in the enquiry form and the team will confirm whether they can attend.

Do you bring your own cleaning equipment and products?

Equipment and cleaning products are selected according to the service and site requirements. Tell us what needs cleaning and we’ll confirm what will be provided as part of your quote.

How do I book a cleaning appointment?

Complete the enquiry form with your contact details, location, preferred service and date. It opens WhatsApp with your information ready to send. Your booking is not confirmed until Hygello replies and agrees the schedule.

How far in advance should I schedule?

Share your preferred date and time as early as possible. Availability is confirmed individually by the Hygello team; submitting an enquiry does not reserve a time slot.

What payment methods do you accept?

Payment arrangements are confirmed directly with you when the service and quote are agreed. Please verify the payment details with Hygello before making a transfer.

Can I request a one-time or recurring cleaning service?

Yes. You can enquire about a one-time clean or recurring daily, weekly or monthly arrangements. The team will confirm the options available for your location and needs.

Can I request a quote before booking?

Absolutely. Send the details of your space and the cleaning required. Hygello can discuss the scope and quote with you before you decide whether to schedule.
